# FirstHey - Lead Capture Platform Features

FirstHey is an enterprise-grade, AI-native Lead Capture platform purpose-built for high-speed data entry at trade shows, exhibitions, and networking events. 

Below is a comprehensive breakdown of the core features and capabilities our platform offers.

---

## ⚡ 1. Intelligent Lead Capture (The 4-Step Wizard)
Designed for the chaos of the exhibition floor, the capture engine allows sales reps to input data in seconds.

*   **AI Business Card OCR:** Simply snap a photo of a business card. The Google Gemini AI engine instantly extracts the Name, Company, Email, Phone, and Job Title with high accuracy.
*   **Rapid Context Notes:** Add quick text notes about the lead's specific interests (e.g., "Interested in Enterprise tier, follow up next week").
*   **AI Voice Transcriptions:** Don't have time to type? Record a quick voice memo after speaking to a lead. The AI will transcribe your voice into structured text and attach it to the lead's profile.
*   **Instant Review & Submit:** Review the AI-extracted data, correct any minor typos, and save the lead instantly to your cloud database.

## 🎯 2. Automated Lead Scoring & Intelligence
Not all leads are created equal. FirstHey helps you prioritize who to contact first.

*   **AI Lead Grading:** The platform automatically analyzes the lead's job title and your context notes to assign a score: **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold**.
*   **Rich Profiles:** Every lead profile displays a clean timeline of when they were captured, which specific exhibition/campaign they belong to, and all attached notes and transcripts.

## ✉️ 3. Adaptive Email Engine (E1S1 / E1S2 Architecture)
Automate your follow-ups so you never lose a potential deal after an event.

*   **Intelligent Subject Rotation:** E1S1 stands for "Email 1, Subject 1". If a client doesn't open the E1S1 email after a few days, the system assumes the subject wasn't catchy enough and automatically sends **E1S2** (the exact same email, but with a different, more attractive subject line).
*   **Cold Lead Filtering:** If a lead continues to ignore emails (e.g., up to E1S3), the system will automatically stop sending to that cold person so you can focus entirely on your Hot leads.
*   **Hot Lead Progression:** If a lead opens Email 1, the system automatically progresses them to the next step in the sequence (E2S1) to keep the conversation moving forward.
*   **Automated Background Queue:** All of this email processing, open-tracking, and subject rotation happens silently in the background.

## 📊 4. Real-Time Analytics Dashboard
Get a bird's-eye view of your exhibition ROI and lead generation progress.

*   **Lead Velocity Tracking:** Track exactly how many leads you are capturing throughout the event.
*   **Conversion Metrics:** View the breakdown of Hot vs. Warm vs. Cold leads for specific campaigns.
*   **Centralized Inbox:** Access all system notifications and email queue statuses directly from your unified dashboard.
